# Welcome to Meridian Universe

A science fiction worldbuilding project mapping the Milky Way — designing star systems, civilizations, and the deep future history of humanity and its neighbours. Everything here is exploratory: ideas, models, and reference material in service of storytelling.

## Site Sections

**About** — Background on the project and its goals.

**Projects** — Design notes for the core in-universe systems: Meridian (the stellar reference database), Worlds, World Maps, Colony Models, and AI-developed software.

**Models** — The mathematics behind the colony growth models, including a summary of the Turchin equations that underpin civilisation dynamics.
